CLOSE THE GAP — WINDOWS DEMO

1. Both players extract this same Windows ZIP.
2. Open the extracted Close-the-Gap folder and run CloseTheGap.exe.
   Keep CloseTheGap.pck beside the executable. No Godot installation needed.
3. Host: Host a game > choose map, rules, weather and Quick/Full > Create game.
4. Copy the private invite and send it privately to your friend.
5. Friend: Join a game > Paste invite > Join friend.
6. Both press Ready. Roles swap every round. Quick is 2 rounds; Full is 6.

Try Crow's Ridge / Paired duel / Wind gusts first. Relay infiltration adds
three sabotage stations and forces decisions about holding or leaving a perch.
The same friend invite flow works through a direct path or encrypted relay.
No router setup or account is required. Public connection service availability
is outside this demo's control; Retry or make a new invite if needed.
Allow network access if Windows Firewall asks. Keep invites private.

CONTROLS (keyboard bindings can be changed in Settings)
WASD + mouse   Move / look      Shift  Sprint      Ctrl  Crouch
Space          Jump             Tab    Map (your position only)
Left mouse     Attack/fire      Right mouse   Aim handgun / scope rifle
1 / 2          Knife / handgun (stalker)       R  Reload
G              Throw pebble (3)               Q  Reflector decoy (1)
T              Tripwire alarm (sniper, 1)     B  Hold binoculars (sniper)
E              Hold sabotage/repair, or use the western handline
F1             Field guide      Esc    Menu (online play continues)

PLAY
Stalker: use cover and crouch to approach from behind. A rear knife hit is
lethal. Frontal knife hits deal 35 damage; handgun hits deal 40.
Sniper: stay on connected high ground, hide, observe and change perches.
A rifle hit is lethal. Reload the 3-round magazine; you have 9 reserve rounds.
Scoping reveals a glint; binocular observation does not. Release B to shoot.
Bushes conceal. Trunks and boulders stop bullets. Birds and sound give rough
clues. Gusts/rain mask footsteps. Mud tracks fade; they show past movement.

RELAY INFILTRATION
Hold E facing a nearby intact station for 4 seconds to sabotage it.
Two offline stations start a 30-second deadline: stalker wins if it expires.
Sniper holds E for 5 seconds to repair. Movement, damage or weapon use cancels
progress. A repaired station stays repaired. Eliminations still win.
Base timer is 150 seconds; an active repair deadline can extend to 180.

AFTER A MATCH
Review any completed round. Save match recording stores both viewpoints on
this PC. Return to menu > Watch > Your recordings to play it back.
Play again returns both friends to the ready room; both must be ready.
The field journal records your actual online visits and role results locally.
Practice and the two weapon lessons are available offline.
